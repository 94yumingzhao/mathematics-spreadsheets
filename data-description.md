# Supplementary spreadsheets

This repository holds Spreadsheets S1 and S2 of the review article "Production Planning in the Steel Industry and Its Downstream Manufacturers: A Review of Models and Solution Approaches".

- `spreadsheet-S1.xlsx` is Spreadsheet S1, with the publication records, coding definitions, literature selection details, and risk of bias assessments.
- `spreadsheet-S2.xlsx` is Spreadsheet S2, with the screening record of the October 2026 searches and the searches of conference proceedings.

The first sheet of each file explains its contents.
The tables cited in the spreadsheets are those of the article.
